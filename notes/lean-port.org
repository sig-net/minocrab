notes/formal-verification-options.org (the ground-truth survey) with the

* 1. The path decision: MODEL-FIRST, extraction as the prepared upgrade

Two ways to get Rust facts into Lean: EXTRACTION (Aeneas: Rust → LLBC →
Lean) or MODELLING (a hand/LLM-written Lean model mirroring the Rust,
kept honest by review and by stating the gap).

DECISION: model-first. The reasons, each checkable:

- The survey's two named Aeneas blockers were both in our passes. ONE IS
  NOW GONE: M23 R4 replaced the passes' =HashMap=/=BTreeMap= with
  =AssocMap= (a =Vec<(String, V)>= with linear scan) for Kani reasons —
  and =Vec= + =String= are exactly what Aeneas's std model covers. The
  OTHER STANDS: =operands_mut='s =Vec<&mut Operand>= (a generic
  instantiated at a mutable reference, documented unsupported).
- Even with both cleared, extraction runs Charon over a crate whose
  dependency tree is the midnight stack; carving an extractable
  sub-crate is real work with no proof content.
- The Agda precedent itself is a model, not an extraction; and the
  LLM-proving sweet spot (§7's Aria result: spec-given lemmas at 75.5%
  first-attempt) is exactly "prove things about a small defined model".

THE PREPARED UPGRADE: a pure =map_operands(&Instruction, impl Fn(&Operand)
-> Operand) -> Instruction= twin beside =operands_mut= would clear the
last extraction blocker; it is the same verification-motivated shape as
the AssocMap change. Do it when (if) extraction is attempted; the model
does not need it.

CONFIRMED by dmd 2026-08-28, after reviewing the tradeoffs: extraction
stays deferred — "this is going to hurt ergonomics too much for now, we
can revisit later." The deciding weight was proof ergonomics (extracted
state-passing definitions vs the clean models, against the LLM-written/
LLM-maintained axis); the preparation steps above stay as recorded.

THE HONEST GAP a model carries, stated once and inherited by every proof:
a Lean model proof warrants the ALGORITHM, and the claim that the Rust
implements that algorithm rests on review plus the Rust-side instruments
(unit tests, the Kani-bounded twins where they close). Extraction would
close that gap; nothing else does. Every Lean file here says so in its
header.

* 4. The ZKIR-pass prong (new Lean)

MODEL: =Instruction= as a small inductive — =copy (dst : Name) (src :
Operand)=, =constrainBits (v : Operand) (bits : Nat)=,
=constrainBoolean=, =output (vals : List Operand)=, and =opaque (touched
: List Operand)= for everything else (the passes treat all other
instructions uniformly: fold substitutes operands, dedup passes through —
so ONE opaque constructor models the whole complement FAITHFULLY for
these two passes; the model file says so and why).

SEMANTICS: for dedup, a stream's meaning per wire is its solution set
(=⋂ {v < 2^b}= = =v < 2^min=); for fold, an environment-passing
evaluator over an abstract value domain with the observable list
(the same shape R4's scoped harness used, unbounded here).

THEOREMS (the R4 specimens, discharged without bounds):
- dedup: output is a subsequence; non-constraints preserved with
  multiplicity; per-wire tightest bound (hence solution set) unchanged;
  idempotence.
- fold, split in two halves because they need different machinery:
  - SYNTACTIC (no side conditions): =output= operand lists verbatim;
    the non-copy skeleton preserved in kind and order; the fold is
    EXACTLY filter-then-substitute (=foldRun_is_filter_map=) — nothing
    is added, reordered, or rewritten beyond operand substitution.
  - SEMANTIC (operand-VALUES preserved under the copy environment):
    requires an SSA well-formedness apparatus — copy destinations
    defined before use, no rebinding — because the Rust substitutes
    globally and only SSA input (guaranteed by Builder3) makes that
    sound. The statement is fixed as a comment at the bottom of
    =Fold.lean= so the goal precedes the machinery.

REFLECTION into the type-level contract (dmd's step 3, applied to the
prong): the honest statement is that Rust's type system cannot make
"preserves the PI stream" a trait bound — the property is semantic. What
it CAN do, and what this designs for: a =VerifiedPass= marker trait,
implementable only via a macro/constructor that names the Lean theorem
file discharging the pass's obligation (a build-time existence check, a
doc-link, and a distinct type the pipeline can REQUIRE:
=run_pipeline_verified(Vec<Box<dyn VerifiedPass>>)=). A third-party pass
without a proof still runs through the ordinary pipeline; it cannot
CLAIM verification, which is as far as the type system reaches — said
so, per FIT's shortfall clause.

- =WellFormed= is exactly what =Builder3= guarantees and no more — copy

  a rebound or use-before-def name really would change meaning.

  RESOLVED; one shared =envStep= is factored out of =namedOf=

  agree definitionally.
- The induction (=observe_foldRun=) runs over the SUFFIX with both
  sides' environments generalized, carrying: folded names still
  undefined are unused (defs-before-uses), already-defined folded names
  agree with the original walk's environment (=namedOf_get_notMem= +
  no-rebinding close the dropped-copy case), and the folded-side
  environment agrees with the original outside =folded='s domain. The
  returned-names filter needs only two facts, proved as implication
  lemmas (=filter_get_some=, =filter_get_none=) — the predicate depends
  only on the key, so a key's entries drop or survive uniformly.

* 6. The limitations discussion (the milestone's honest-writeup bullet)

The record PARITY is measured against, stated bluntly so nobody scopes
this work on the Agda's reputation. Public-facing summary in
VERIFICATION.md §5; this section is the full account.

** What the Compact repo's spec actually is

9,194 lines of Agda across 21 files, of which the CI-checked core is
the Lsrc STATIC/TYPING semantics (~4,700 lines) plus =Coverage.agda=.
Within that checked core, at our pinned rev:

- WELL-FORMEDNESS PLUMBING IS REAL: the syntax is fully represented
  (generated from the compiler's own grammar), ~20 inductive typing
  relations exist, and =Coverage.agda= (=--safe=) machine-checks that
  the typing relation covers every syntax constructor. That last item
  is genuine and we stole the idea (exhaustive matches in =passes.rs= /
  =taint.rs= / =returned_identifiers=).
- THE SEMANTIC CONTENT IS LARGELY ABSENT where it would matter to us:
  arithmetic result types are =⊢undeclared= stubs ("TODO: define bound
  computation"); subtyping is =refl=/=trans= with "TODO: define
  subtyping" and a load-bearing =postulate=; =Castable= is one
  constructor over that stub; =disclose= types through with no
  visibility judgment anywhere — the witness-taint discipline Compact
  actually enforces lives in the compiler, OUTSIDE the spec.
- =Lsrc.lagda.md= carries =--allow-unsolved-metas= (not =--safe=), 29
  TODOs, and describes language 0.16.103 — nine minors behind the
  compiler we pin.
- The OPERATIONAL semantics is an unchecked sketch (=fetch = {!!}=, no
  importer); there is NOTHING about ZKIR, lowering, or optimisation.
- Bycatch of reading closely: =⊢>== is defined via =⊢expr-<== — a
  copy-paste bug in checked code, harmless to their theorems (there
  are none about comparisons) but indicative: a spec nothing consumes
  does not get read.

** What parity therefore means, and where we stand

Their formal coverage that is REAL: syntax representation, typing-rule
plumbing, constructor coverage. Ours: the coverage idea is adopted as
build-breaking exhaustive matches; the typing rules that carry content
(numeric bounds, casts, visibility) exist in our TYPE SYSTEM as
enforcement (const asserts, =Meet=, the disclose gate, Public-only
=CircuitOut=) with Lean warrants BEHIND them (=MinocrabStdProofs=) —
which is coverage their spec does not have: their bound computation is
undeclared, ours is proven sound AND minimal; their disclose rule is
transparent, our gate discipline is proven sound and complete over the
model. Where their spec has nothing and so do our types, the OTHER
APPROACHES hold the line and are named in §3's table (rows 4-6:
artifact-agreement tests, instruction-level differentials,
byte-equality twins + the survey's fixed bug classes).

Beyond parity, on ground they do not cover at all: the ZKIR pass layer
(=MinocrabProofs=, six syntactic + one semantic + four dedup theorems,
reflected as =VerifiedPass=) and the Kani-bounded interval arithmetic
under the taint lint.

** What our Lean is NOT, restated

Every proof here is a MODEL proof: it warrants the algorithm/rule as
transcribed, and the transcription-matches-Rust claim rests on review
plus the Rust-side instruments (each file's header says so). No
extraction, no proof about the running binary, and no operational
semantics of ZKIR on either side. And the proofs are LLM-written and
LLM-maintained under review — the LLM-writability axis was the design
constraint, not a caveat discovered later.
