#+title: What sig-net/mpc re-implemented, and what moved upstream (2026-11-01)

* The finding
Reviewing minocrab PR #18 (Pessina, "Port the signer to v0.25.0 and make minocrab linkable from mpc") against the mpc branch that links it (=sig-net/mpc= =feat/midnight-native-publisher=, =e31a02e4=) showed mpc linking =minocrab-publisher= but not =signet-protocol=, and carrying its own copies of four things the workspace was built to be the one definition of: the Misc envelope's 32/256 split and the three padded event names (=chain-midnight/src/emissions.rs=), the off-circuit protocol hashes (=compact-hashing/src/lib.rs=, =chain-midnight/src/hashing.rs=: request id, EVM type-2 transaction digest, attested output hash, attestation digest), the =SignBidirectionalEventV1= record as plain data (=chain-midnight/src/records.rs=), and the inverse of the publisher's three =*_misc= encoders.

The copies had already crossed once in the other direction: =crates/signet-sim= is a translation of mpc's reader and =compact-hashing= at mpc =10360c3c= (2026-09-05), and it is at the pre-0.25.0 digest (output limbs hashed directly, no =outputHash=; =executionDest= as =Bytes<32>= where v0.25.0 has =Bytes<64>=). So both sides held a stale copy of the other's hashing. mpc's copy is tested against a fixture of compiled-Compact vectors it captured; minocrab's is tested against the eDSL circuits; neither against the other.

* What moved, and where (branch =upstream-mpc-protocol=, off PR #18's head =4575b94=)
- =signet-protocol= (no new dependencies): =misc::MISC_NAME_LEN= / =MISC_PAYLOAD_LEN=, =misc::padded_name= (a =const fn=), =misc::split=; =circuits::*_EVENT_NAME= as the padded 32 bytes and =circuits::circuit_of_event_name= as the inverse of =event_name=; the protocol enums =HashDomain=, =OutputKind=, =TxParamType=, =SignatureAlgorithm=, =SignatureDestination= with =TryFrom<u8>= and =ALL=. The vault port's =signet_v1::hash_domain= and =output_kind= constants now cast these rather than restating the numbers.
- =signet-hashing= (new, =publish = false= like every =midnight-*= dependent): =request_id=, =evm_type2_tx_params_digest=, =attested_output_hash=, =attestation_digest=, =Attestation::over= and =digest_is_consistent=, the plain-data =EvmType2TxParams= / =EvmCalldata= / =EvmAccessListEntry= / =RequestIdPreimage= / =SignBidirectionalRecord= / =TxParams=, =ETHEREUM_CAIP2_ID= padded to 64. Hashing is the pinned ledger's =transient_hash= over its =FieldRepr=, whose =[u8]= packing is exactly the FAB alignment (the =N mod 31= leftover first, then 31-byte limbs from the top: =[hi, lo]= for a =Bytes<32>=, three limbs for a =Bytes<64>=) — pinned by a unit test, so the crate adds no packing of its own.
- =minocrab-publisher=: =call::decode_misc= → =SignerEvent= (strict: exact length, one of the three padded names, zeros past the last field), =SignerEvent::misc= as its inverse, =Attestation::over= / =Attestation::typed= bridging to =signet_hashing::Attestation=, =pub use signet_hashing as hashing=, =PublishError::NotASignerEvent=.

* The oracle
=crates/signet-hashing/tests/fixtures/api-parity-vectors.json= is mpc's capture of the compiled circuits (compactc 0.33.0-rc.2 over =@sig-net/midnight@0.25.0-rc.1= =350f9a3=; the hashing is unchanged at v0.25.0): nine request vectors as the record's FAB atoms with widths (shapes =<1,0,0>=, =<0,0,0>=, =<1,2,2>=, =<2,2,3>=, with partly used slots), ten attestation vectors, and one =respondBidirectional= emission with its fields. =signet-hashing/tests/compact_oracle.rs= decodes each record from its atoms and matches every request id and transaction digest, every output hash and digest, the SDK's own domain vector, and the emitted event's digest; =minocrab-publisher/tests/misc_decode.rs= decodes the emission byte-for-byte to its fields and re-encodes it to the same bytes. No Rust hash took part in producing the expected bytes. The pure circuits themselves are compiled =--skip-zk= in the corpus (=circuits.compact= has no zkir), so a direct simulator differential is not available for them; the eDSL ports in =signet_v1= are at 0.24.1 (twelve-limb request id), so they are not the v0.25.0 oracle either.

* Still open
- =signet-sim= stays at its 0.24.1 vintage because the vault (=signet_v1=, =erc20_vault=) is; when the vault moves to 0.25.0, =signet_sim::hashing::v1= should become calls into =signet-hashing= and =signet_sim::records= should become =signet_hashing::SignBidirectionalRecord=.
- The record DECODER (ledger cell → =SignBidirectionalRecord=) is still mpc's (=chain-midnight/src/reader.rs=) and =signet-sim='s (=reader.rs=, 0.24.1). The fixture test decodes from atoms, not from a =StateValue=; a =StateValue= decoder beside =signet-hashing='s record would be the next move, and =minocrab-ledger= already knows the alignment.
- mpc's =midnight-respond-codec= (the Borsh output encoding, mirroring the SDK's =executedEvmRespondOutput=) is SDK-shaped and was left where it is.
- The mpc side: replace =compact-hashing= with =signet-hashing=, =emissions.rs='s constants with =signet-protocol='s, and its =Attestation= construction with =Attestation::over=. Not done here; it is mpc's PR.
